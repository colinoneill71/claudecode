Case Study

Empowering a Life in Motion:
Unifying Experience Through Journey Design

Client Major Membership Organization
Role Strategic Journey Program Lead
Focus UX, Content, Benefits, Digital Services

Members of this major organization weren't looking for more features. They were looking for relevance. Despite offering valuable benefits, robust digital services, and extensive content, the organization struggled with fragmentation. Each team operated independently, creating features without consideration for the broader member journey. The result: overwhelmed members who couldn't find what mattered to them, and declining engagement with programs that should have been driving value.

The challenge wasn't building more. It was creating coherence from what already existed and designing experiences that adapt to each member's unique life stage, activities, and goals.

The Challenge: Fragmentation Hiding Value

The organization had invested heavily in digital capabilities—mobile apps, web platforms, benefit programs, content libraries. But these investments operated in silos. UX teams designed interfaces without coordination with content strategy. Benefits programs launched without integration into digital touchpoints. Features accumulated without a unifying vision of member journeys.

What members experienced:

A navigation structure that reflected internal org charts rather than their needs. Benefits they qualified for but never discovered. Content buried in separate properties they didn't know existed. Apps that duplicated features without talking to each other. Every interaction required them to understand the organization's structure rather than the organization understanding their lives.

The Approach: Journey-Led Transformation

Rather than adding more features, we led a strategic journey program to unify the fragmented experience. The goal was creating coherence: connecting UX, content, benefits, and digital services around the journeys that actually mattered to members' lives.

  • Foundational Design System

    We implemented a design system that created visual and experiential consistency across all digital properties. But more importantly, it established patterns for how features and services integrate into journeys rather than existing as standalone tools. The system became the connective tissue that made siloed programs feel like one coherent membership.

  • Journey-Oriented Service Integration

    We mapped key member journeys and reorganized features, content, and benefits around those experiences. Planning a trip? The journey brought together insurance benefits, travel content, location-based offers, and booking tools in one integrated flow. Pursuing wellness goals? The journey connected activity tracking, rewards programs, educational content, and community features as one experience.

  • Personalization Platform Architecture

    We defined a personalization architecture with 12 user states and 14 activity metrics, enabling 168 unique personalized experiences across all digital properties. This wasn't personalization for its own sake—it was ensuring that every member saw what mattered most to them, when it mattered most, without having to hunt for it.

  • Mobile App Suite Activation

    We activated previously siloed programs as a unified suite of mobile apps, each focused on specific journey moments while sharing common design patterns, data, and member context. Apps that used to compete for attention now worked together to support members throughout their journeys.

Why It Matters: Coherence Drives Engagement

The transformation wasn't about technology. It was about respecting members' time and intelligence by organizing services around their lives rather than the organization's structure.

Members don't want to learn your org chart. They want you to understand their lives and make it easy to get value from their membership.

26% Increase in members earning rewards for primary activities
168 Unique personalized experiences across digital properties
Unified Previously siloed teams and touchpoints

The work delivered measurable engagement impact—a 26% increase in members earning rewards points for primary activities. But the broader outcome was creating a roadmap and operating model that aligned previously siloed teams around member journeys rather than internal projects.

The personalization platform architecture became the foundation for future innovation, enabling the organization to continue evolving experiences without recreating fragmentation. When teams share common journey understanding and technical patterns, new capabilities reinforce coherence rather than adding complexity.
