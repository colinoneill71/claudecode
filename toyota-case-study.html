Product Case Study

Toyota Connected Services:
Intelligent Vehicle Platform

Client Toyota
Role Product Strategy & UX Design Lead
Focus Connected Vehicles, Service Platform, Mobile Experience
Impact Multi-vehicle line platform adoption

This case study showcases product strategy and UX design for an automotive connected services platform that integrates vehicle data, mobile applications, and dealer systems. The platform creates an intelligent ecosystem connecting vehicles, drivers, and service centers.

Modern vehicle owners expect seamless digital experiences that extend beyond the dashboard. The challenge was creating a cohesive product that unifies in-vehicle systems, mobile applications, and service touchpoints into one connected experience.

The Opportunity

Connected vehicle technology generates valuable data about vehicle health, driving patterns, and maintenance needs. However, this data only creates value when translated into actionable insights and convenient services for vehicle owners.

Multiple Vehicle systems integrated
Seamless Mobile to vehicle handoff
Proactive Service recommendations

Product Design Approach

  • Service Architecture

    Designed a comprehensive service architecture that connects vehicle telemetry, mobile applications, dealer systems, and customer service platforms into one intelligent ecosystem.

  • Mobile-First Design

    Led UX design for native mobile applications that provide remote vehicle access, trip tracking, maintenance alerts, and dealer service scheduling from anywhere.

  • Predictive Maintenance

    Created product features that translate vehicle data into proactive maintenance recommendations, reducing unexpected breakdowns and improving service planning.

  • Dealer Integration

    Designed seamless handoffs between customer-facing digital tools and dealer service systems, ensuring information continuity throughout the ownership experience.

Key Platform Capabilities

The connected services platform delivers several core capabilities that enhance the ownership experience:

  • Remote Vehicle Access

    Mobile-controlled features including remote start, climate control, door locks, and vehicle location tracking provide convenience and peace of mind.

  • Health Monitoring

    Real-time vehicle diagnostics and health monitoring that alert owners to maintenance needs before they become problems, with direct scheduling links to preferred dealers.

  • Journey Intelligence

    Trip logging, driving insights, and route optimization features that help owners understand their vehicle usage patterns and improve efficiency.

  • Service Ecosystem

    Integrated platform connecting roadside assistance, dealer service scheduling, maintenance history, and warranty information in one accessible system.

Design System & Scalability

Established comprehensive design patterns and interaction frameworks that enable consistent experiences across multiple vehicle lines and model years. The design system supports rapid feature expansion while maintaining brand consistency.
