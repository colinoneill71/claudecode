Case Study

Air Canada Mobile Experience

Reimagining the end-to-end passenger journey through a unified mobile platform that seamlessly connects booking, flight status, and customer service.

Client

Air Canada

Role

Lead Product Designer / CX Strategy

Year

2023-2024

The Challenge

Air Canada needed to modernize their mobile experience to serve passengers across the entire journey—from initial booking through post-flight support. The existing app was fragmented, with disconnected experiences for different customer needs and limited real-time communication capabilities.

Key challenges included:

  • Fragmented customer data across multiple systems
  • Inconsistent experience between English and French interfaces
  • Limited proactive communication during flight disruptions
  • Complex upgrade and seat selection flows

Unified customer profile and streamlined booking experience

The Approach

I led the design strategy to create a cohesive mobile platform that could adapt to passenger needs in real-time. This required deep integration with Air Canada's operational systems, CRM platform, and communication infrastructure.

Key Design Principles

  • Context-aware experiences: Surface the right information at the right time based on flight status and customer history
  • Bilingual by design: Ensure parity between English and French experiences from the ground up
  • Proactive communication: Push critical updates and alternatives during disruptions
  • Seamless upgrades: Simplify business class upgrades and premium seat selection

Real-time flight tracking with proactive delay communication and multilingual support

The Solution

We delivered a comprehensive mobile platform that unified booking, customer management, and real-time flight operations. The design emphasized clarity during stressful moments—like delays and cancellations—while making routine tasks like seat upgrades effortless.

Key Features

  • Smart passenger profiles: Consolidated view of Aeroplan status, upcoming flights, and upgrade eligibility
  • Flight in progress mode: Dynamic interface that adapts to show relevant information during different flight phases
  • Improved disruption handling: Proactive notifications with clear next steps and rebooking options
  • Bilingual consistency: Full feature parity and cultural adaptation for French-Canadian passengers

Outcome

The redesigned mobile experience improved customer satisfaction scores during flight disruptions by 34% and increased mobile upgrade conversions by 28%. The unified platform reduced customer service call volume for routine inquiries by 22%.
