Product Case Study

AARP Digital Platform:
Unifying Member Experience

Client AARP
Role Product Design Lead
Focus Platform Design, Member Engagement, Digital Transformation
Impact 38M+ members, Unified digital experience

This case study demonstrates product design and strategy leadership for a next-generation member engagement platform serving 38 million members. The challenge was transforming legacy systems into a unified digital experience that integrates benefits, advocacy tools, and personalized content delivery.

Members needed seamless access to their benefits, educational resources, and community engagement opportunities across multiple digital touchpoints. The existing fragmented systems created confusion and reduced member engagement with key services and programs.

The Challenge

AARP's digital ecosystem had evolved organically over decades, resulting in siloed experiences across web properties, mobile applications, and member services. Members encountered different interfaces, inconsistent information architecture, and disconnected data systems when trying to access benefits or engage with content.

15+ Disparate digital touchpoints
38M Members to serve
Multiple Legacy system integrations

Product Strategy & Design Approach

  • Unified Platform Architecture

    Designed a comprehensive product architecture that creates a single, cohesive member experience across all digital touchpoints. The platform integrates member data, benefits information, and content delivery into one intelligent system.

  • Personalization Framework

    Developed a personalization engine that tailors content and benefits recommendations based on member demographics, interests, and engagement patterns. This ensures members discover relevant services and opportunities.

  • Design System Foundation

    Created a scalable design system with reusable components, patterns, and guidelines that enable consistent experiences across all member touchpoints while supporting rapid feature development.

  • Mobile-First Experience

    Led the product strategy for native mobile applications that provide on-the-go access to key member benefits, ID cards, discounts, and community features.

Key Product Features

The platform includes several core product capabilities designed to enhance member value and engagement:

  • Member Dashboard

    A personalized home base that surfaces relevant benefits, content, and opportunities based on individual member profiles and preferences.

  • Benefits Discovery

    Intelligent tools that help members discover and activate benefits they might not know about, increasing the value they receive from membership.

  • Content Personalization

    Dynamic content delivery that adapts to member interests, life stage, and engagement history across articles, videos, and educational resources.

Impact & Outcomes

The unified digital platform established a foundation for long-term member engagement and platform scalability. The product architecture and design system created efficiency in development while improving member experience quality.

Unified Platform architecture delivered
Scalable Design system established
Personalized Member experiences enabled

Product design for member organizations requires balancing diverse needs while creating coherent, scalable systems that serve millions.
