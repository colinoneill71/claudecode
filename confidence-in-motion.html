Case Study

Confidence in Motion:
Connecting Vehicles, Agents, and Data

Client Global Auto Rental Company
Role CX Strategy & Service Design Lead
Focus AI-Powered Service Architecture
Impact New GTM play, significant pipeline impact

Travelers don't lose confidence because of unexpected problems. They lose confidence when they can't get straight answers about what's happening, what their options are, and who's going to help them. For a global auto rental company serving millions of travelers annually, this gap between expectation and reality was creating friction at every touchpoint.

The business had invested heavily in fleet technology, customer apps, and AI capabilities. But these systems operated in isolation. Vehicles knew about maintenance issues that agents couldn't see. Agents had customer context that operations couldn't access. Data lived in separate platforms that never talked to each other. The result: travelers asking basic questions that nobody had the tools to answer confidently.

The Challenge: Fragmented Systems, Frustrated Travelers

The symptoms were visible everywhere. Counter agents manually checking multiple systems to find vehicle availability. Travelers receiving conflicting information from the app versus the location staff. Maintenance issues discovered mid-rental with no proactive communication. Every interaction reinforced the same message: the systems weren't designed to work together, and the customer paid the price.

What we discovered through research:

The company had all the right pieces—connected vehicles, sophisticated CRM, operational intelligence platforms—but no service architecture connecting them into a coherent experience. Agents felt undermined by systems that knew more than they did. Customers felt stranded when problems arose because nobody had visibility into the full picture.

The Solution: Service Architecture That Connects Everything

Rather than building another standalone tool, we designed a service architecture that unified vehicles, agents, and data into one coherent system. The goal wasn't automation for its own sake—it was enabling proactive assistance that restores traveler confidence when things go wrong.

  • Connected Intelligence

    We integrated vehicle telemetry, customer profiles, and operational data into a unified platform that gives agents complete context in real-time. When a vehicle reports a maintenance flag, agents know before the customer calls. When a flight is delayed, the system automatically extends pickup windows and notifies both traveler and location.

  • Proactive Service Recovery

    Instead of waiting for complaints, the system identifies friction points and triggers interventions. A vehicle running low on fuel near the return location gets flagged for waived refueling fees. A traveler with a history of upgrades gets automatic priority when availability opens up. Problems get resolved before they become customer service moments.

  • Agent Empowerment Through Context

    Agents no longer need to piece together information from five different systems. They see the full journey: travel context, vehicle status, service history, and available options—all in one interface. This transforms them from transaction processors into travel partners who can actually solve problems.

Why It Matters: Trust Through Transparency

This wasn't just about operational efficiency. It was about rebuilding traveler confidence through transparency and proactive support. When systems work together, travelers get answers before they have to ask. When problems arise, they're met with solutions instead of excuses.

Confidence comes from knowing someone has visibility into your situation and the authority to help. The best service experiences don't require customers to explain their problems—the system already knows.

Reusable Patterns for future AI initiatives

The service architecture became more than a customer experience improvement—it became a differentiator in market. The patterns we established for connecting vehicles, agents, and data created a foundation for future AI-powered service innovations across the enterprise.
